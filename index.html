Cyberpunk 2077

Action RPG Open World Single Player

Step into Night City, a sprawling dystopian metropolis dominated by corporations and advanced technology, as V, a fully customizable character. The game blends first-person shooting, role-playing and open-world exploration, letting you choose your own playstyle, whether that's stealth, hacking or direct combat.

A branching narrative with choices that shape the story's outcome gives it plenty of replay value. It had a bumpy launch, but it has since been polished into a very fun game with a huge amount of content to explore.
