About Us

What this site is

Games Review Page is a place for honest reviews of games, covering what works, what doesn't, and whether a game is worth your time.

What I review

I review a wide range of games across various categories, with a focus on role-playing games in different settings and genres. My reviews cover both single-player and multiplayer experiences, with insights into gameplay mechanics, storylines, graphics and overall enjoyment.

How I review

Every game is played properly before it gets a review. I look at gameplay, story, visuals and how polished the game feels.

Who's behind it
