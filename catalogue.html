Cyberpunk 2077

Action RPG Open World Single Player

Cyberpunk 2077 is a game that takes place in a dystopian future. The setting of it is a sprawling metropolis called Night City, where players assume the role of a customizable character known as V. The game offers a vast open world filled with diverse districts, each with its own unique atmosphere and culture. Players can explore the city, engage in various activities, and interact with a wide range of characters, all while navigating the challenges of a cyberpunk society dominated by corporations and advanced technology.

The gameplay is a blend of first-person shooting, role-playing elements, and open-world exploration. Players can choose different playstyles, such as stealth, hacking, or direct combat, and can customize their character's abilities and appearance. The game features a branching narrative with multiple choices that impact the story's outcome, allowing for replayability and different experiences based on player decisions.

Overall... the game is very well polished after its bumpy launch and has redeemed itself as a game. It is very fun and has a lot of content to explore. The setting, the environment and the characters are all very well curated, and the game is very fun to get into.

Ghost of Tsushima

Action Adventure Single Player

Ghost of Tsushima is an action-adventure game set in feudal Japan. The story follows Jin Sakai, a samurai who must defend his homeland from an invading force. The game features a beautiful open world with diverse environments and a compelling narrative.

Jin Sakai is a lone samurai who defends his homeland while straying from the path of the samurai, slowly losing his honor and identity slowly losing his samurai honor and identity.

The game also features a rich soundtrack and stunning visuals that enhance the playthrough and make it so there's never a boring moment.

The gameplay emphasizes stealth and combat, allowing players to approach situations in various ways. The game also includes a robust character progression system and a variety of side quests to enhance the player's experience. Overall, Ghost of Tsushima is a visually stunning and immersive game that offers a unique perspective on the samurai era.

Titanfall 2

Shooter Multiplayer Single Player

Titanfall 2 is a first-person shooter game that features fast-paced gameplay and a unique blend of infantry and mech combat. The game is set in a futuristic universe where players control pilots who can call down powerful mechs known as Titans to aid them in battle. The game offers both a single-player campaign and multiplayer modes, providing a variety of experiences for players.

The single-player campaign follows the story of a pilot named Jack Cooper and his Titan, BT-7274, as they navigate through a series of missions and challenges. The campaign is praised for its engaging story, well-designed levels, and innovative gameplay mechanics that allow players to seamlessly switch between pilot and Titan combat.

The game was well-received by critics and players for its innovative approach to movement and combat while implementing a secondary storyline and playstyle of titans.
